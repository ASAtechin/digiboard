**App name:** DigiBoard by Shiksha Tantra

**Short description (80 characters):** Your school's timetable, cover and notices on every classroom and corridor screen.

**Full description:**

DigiBoard turns a TV or display in each classroom and corridor into the school's live board. It shows the period on now and what is next, today's substitutions and room changes, the school's notices, and practice drills the moment the school starts one, with the all-clear when it ends.

- Paired once by the school's admin with a short code; nobody signs in on the screen.
- Keeps showing the saved timetable through a network cut.
- Starts by itself when the screen is switched on.
- Shows teachers' names, subjects and rooms only: never contact details, never anything about a student.

You need a school that uses Shiksha Tantra. The school's admin pairs each screen.
