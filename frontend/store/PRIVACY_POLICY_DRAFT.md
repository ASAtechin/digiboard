The DigiBoard display app runs on screens in schools that use Shiksha Tantra. It shows the school's timetable, substitutions, notices and practice drills. **[School name]** ("the school") decides what its screens show and is responsible for it. **[Shiksha Tantra legal entity]** ("Shiksha Tantra", "we") provides the app and the service, on the school's written instructions.

## Nobody signs in on a screen

A screen is paired once by a school admin, with a short code shown on the screen. From then on it holds only its own screen key. Nobody signs in on it, and it collects nothing about the people in front of it: no camera, microphone, location, contacts or files, no advertising, analytics or crash reporting.

## What the screen sends, and why

| What | Why |
|---|---|
| A pairing request and, while waiting, the pairing code's check | To be paired by the school's admin |
| A regular check-in: that the screen is on, its mode, the app version, when it started, its local clock, and a fingerprint and short summary of what it is showing (for example that a drill is on screen) | So the school can see its screens are working and that a drill reached every screen |

## What it shows

Teachers' names, subjects and rooms in the timetable and substitutions; the school's notices; drills and their all-clear. **Never contact details of staff, and never anything about an individual student**: the service refuses to send it to screens, and schools are asked not to put it in notices.

## What stays on the screen

The screen key, and a copy of the last timetable and notices, so the screen keeps showing them through a network cut. Revoking the screen in the school's admin, or clearing the app's data, removes them.

## Who can see it, where and how long

As in the school's privacy notice for staff: the school's staff by role; Shiksha Tantra's staff only to run and support the service, with the school's time-limited permission; the hosting providers Vercel and Neon under contract.
