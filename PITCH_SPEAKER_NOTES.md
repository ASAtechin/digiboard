# DigiBoard Pitch Script & Speaker Notes

## Slide 1: Title
"Good morning everyone. We are here to talk about transforming the most overlooked real estate in your school: the classroom door. Introducing DigiBoard."

## Slide 2: The Challenge
"Right now, most schools rely on static paper timetables or basic digital clocks. This creates a 'Black Box' effect. Parents and inspectors walk down your hallways and see closed doors. They have no idea of the incredible value being delivered inside. And if you *do* have digital screens, they often show error messages the moment the internet flickers."

## Slide 3: The Solution
"DigiBoard changes that. It is an autonomous broadcast system. It doesn't just tell time; it tells the story of your academic day."

## Slide 4: For the School (Admin)
"For you as administrators, the biggest benefit is **Reliability**. We've built a 'Zero-Downtime' feature. If your school Wi-Fi goes down, DigiBoard keeps working. You will never see a blank screen or an error message. It also modernizes your campus instantly—showcasing your faculty's qualifications right on the door, proving to visitors that you hire the best."

## Slide 5: For Students
"For students, it provides **Focus**. We've replaced generic course names with specific 'Learning Outcomes'. Before a student even enters the room, they know they aren't just learning 'Math'—they are learning 'Integration Techniques'. This sets the mental stage for success and reduces anxiety about what to expect."

## Slide 6: For Parents
"For parents, DigiBoard equals **Trust**. When they walk through your school for an open house and see structured lesson plans and professional teacher profiles on every door, they feel assured. They see a school that is organized, transparent, and proud of its curriculum."

## Slide 7: Key Capabilities
"It runs itself. It tracks attendance visually to encourage participation. And again, it works offline. It is low maintenance, high impact."

## Slide 8: Conclusion
"DigiBoard is the easiest way to upgrade your school's infrastructure and image simultaneously. Let's bring your hallways to life."
